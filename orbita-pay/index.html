Interactive prototype · Agentic AI UX · Human-in-the-loop

Agent-assisted merchant onboarding

A five-step merchant application where an AI assistant works beside the form. The agent assists. The human decides. The assistant prepares drafts and proposals, the system checks them, and the merchant confirms every one. Legal declarations, bank details and final submission always stay with the merchant.

Simulated assistant: no AI model is connected. Orbita Pay is a fictional brand.
